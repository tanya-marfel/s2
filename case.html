How these numbers work
S2 (Regulation 883/2004): your home health insurer pre-approves treatment abroad and settles the bill with the clinic's country. You pay little or nothing at the clinic, but you must be approved before you go.

Pay and claim back (Directive 2011/24/EU): you pay the clinic yourself, then your insurer repays up to what the same treatment costs at home. You need the money first, and any difference is yours.
